AMRIT

Advanced Manufacturing Research, Innovation and Training Centre

A Joint Centre of Excellence between IIT Hyderabad and Deakin University, Australia

Partnership Overview

IIT Hyderabad and Deakin University, Australia have formally joined hands through a signed Letter of Intent to establish the Joint Centre of Excellence in Advanced Manufacturing Research, Innovation and Training (AMRIT).

This strategic partnership aims to foster cutting-edge research, innovation, and high-impact training in the field of Advanced Manufacturing and Materials. AMRIT will serve as a collaborative platform bringing together leading researchers, academic institutions, industry partners, and R&D organizations from both India and Australia.

Key Objectives of AMRIT

Collaborative Research

Promote joint research initiatives focused on next-generation manufacturing technologies and advanced materials.

Innovation & Industry Impact

Develop innovative, scalable solutions to address real-world challenges in manufacturing, with strong industry relevance.

Capacity Building

Design and implement training programs to equip students, researchers, and professionals with the skills required for the future of advanced manufacturing.

Global Partnerships

Strengthen international academic and industrial collaborations between India and Australia in the manufacturing sector.

Impact & Vision

The AMRIT Centre will not only accelerate technological advancements but will also play a pivotal role in nurturing the next generation of innovators, engineers, and thought leaders in advanced manufacturing.

Through this partnership, we envision creating a hub of excellence that bridges the gap between cutting-edge research and practical industry applications, fostering innovation that addresses global manufacturing challenges.
